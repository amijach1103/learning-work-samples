# Get Ready for the Claude Workshop
## Setup Guide + Sample Prompts

**Time needed:** 15-20 minutes | **Help:** Message Agueda on Teams

---

## What You're Setting Up

Claude AI (desktop app or claude.ai) with two integrations:
- **Asana:** Claude can read and update your tasks
- **Google Drive:** Claude can read your documents

---

## Setup Steps

### 1. Access Claude
- Download the desktop app: [claude.ai/download](https://claude.ai/download) OR use [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) in browser
- Sign in with your **work email**
- Make sure you see your organization's workspace

### 2. Connect Asana
- Click your profile icon → **Settings** → **Integrations**
- Find **Asana** → Click **Connect**
- Authorize when prompted
- Test it: *"What Asana workspaces do I have access to?"*

### 3. Connect Google Drive
- Same place: Settings → Integrations
- Find **Google Drive** → Click **Connect**
- Authorize when prompted
- Test it: *"What documents have I worked on recently?"*

## Sample Prompts to Try

| What you want | What to say |
|---------------|-------------|
| See your week | "What's due this week in Asana?" |
| Status update | "Write a status update based on my Asana tasks" |
| Mark complete | "Mark [task name] as complete in Asana" |
| Add a task | "Add a task to [project]: [task description]" |
| Summarize a doc | "Summarize [document name] from my Google Drive" |
| Find a file | "Find documents related to [topic] in my Drive" |

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
|---------|----------|
| Can't see integrations | Make sure you're in your organization's workspace, not personal |
| Connection fails | Disconnect → Reconnect in Settings |
| Wrong Asana workspace | Tell Claude: "Use my [Workspace Name] workspace" |
| Still stuck? | Message Agueda on Teams. Don't spend more than 10 min blocked |
