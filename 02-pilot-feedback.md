# Pilot feedback log

**Work sample, Agueda Schwartz.** Before the workshop, I asked five colleagues to try the workflow on their own work for about two and a half weeks, with a chat channel for questions and an optional weekly drop-in. I checked in with each of them and logged what they told me, January 2026. The last section is what I changed in the workshop because of it. Names are replaced with numbers; four of the five sent feedback in time.

---

## Participant 1

**Setup**
- Took a bit; had to disconnect and reconnect Google Drive
- Ultimately went smoothly

**What they tried**
- Writing and annotating code for a statistical analysis
- Pulling action items from a meeting notes doc into Asana tasks
- Managing Asana (marking tasks complete)
- Personal use, inspired by the work setup

**What worked**
- Loves the Google Drive, Asana and Claude integration
- Hands-off task creation that doesn't interrupt their flow
- Asana accurate, and nice not to switch tabs
- The code matched published methods

**What was frustrating**
- Lag time: pulling action items takes longer than doing it yourself
- Accuracy: missed several action items it should have added
- For basic things ("what tasks do I have"), it's faster to just open Asana
- Need to be very specific. Claude isn't a mind-reader.

**Blockers**
- Precision required; hasn't tried open-ended project management tasks yet

**In their words**
> "Asana accurate and nice not to switch between tabs. BUT for basic things (what tasks do I have coming up) it takes much longer than just clicking open Asana and looking at 'my tasks.' Can do (somewhat) pulling incomplete action items from meeting notes and put in Asana, but missed several it should have added."

## Themes

- **Integration value is real.** "I love the integration" (Participant 1); status reports were "very good" (Participant 4).
- **Good first pass.** Extracted deliverables well, in a decent format to modify (Participant 2).
- **Speed tradeoff.** Hands-off, but slower than doing it by hand for simple tasks (Participant 1).
- **Accuracy gaps.** Missed action items in meeting notes (Participant 1); didn't pull all the tasks out (Participant 4).
- **Specificity matters.** Needs precise prompts, not open-ended asks (Participant 1); already improving their prompts (Participant 4).
- **Flow.** Value in not breaking your work to switch tabs (Participant 1).
- **Google Drive is read-only.** Can't create or edit docs in Drive (Participants 2 and 3).
- **Templates.** Can't use slide templates (Participant 3).
- **Time is a blocker.** Using it for real work, not experimenting (Participant 3).
- **An integration people wanted isn't available yet**, with potential big wins (Participant 2).
- **The account matters.** One work email didn't work; the other did (Participant 3).

---

## What I changed in the workshop

- Frame Claude as "hands-off," not "faster"
- Teach prompt refinement, because specificity matters (one participant was already iterating)
- Set realistic expectations on accuracy: it won't catch everything
- Show when AI helps and when doing it by hand is better
- Be clear about the Google Drive limits (read-only, no templates)
- Make sure everyone's account is set up on the right email before the workshop
